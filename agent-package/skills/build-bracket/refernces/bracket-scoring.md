# Bracket Scoring Reference

## Points Table

| Prediction | Points |
|---|---|
| Correct Round of 32 winner | +5 |
| Correct Round of 16 winner | +8 |
| Correct quarterfinal winner | +12 |
| Correct semifinal winner | +18 |
| Correct champion | +30 |

## Expected Value by Round

The champion pick alone is worth more than winning 6 Round of 32 picks combined (+30 vs +30). Prioritise your champion research.

Getting semifinalists right (2 picks × 18 = 36) is almost as valuable as the champion alone. Focus analysis on the final four.

## Team Evaluation Checklist

For each team still in the tournament, assess:

- [ ] Goals scored per match (attacking threat)
- [ ] Goals conceded per match (defensive solidity)
- [ ] Biggest win margin (dominant form signal)
- [ ] Key player injury/suspension status
- [ ] Number of matches that went to extra time (fatigue risk)
- [ ] Opponent quality in path so far

## Upset Criteria

An upset pick is worth considering if:
- The underdog has better per-match metrics than their ranking suggests.
- The favourite has injury issues or had a very tough recent match.
- The fixture is a derby or rivalry with historically unpredictable outcomes.

Do not pick upsets without evidence. A wrong Round of 32 upset costs you only 0 (you just miss +5), but a wrong upset in the semifinals costs you potential champion path picks.

## Champion Shortlist Template

Rank top 4 teams by: (goals_scored_per_match × 2) + (1 / max(goals_conceded_per_match, 0.5))

The team with the highest combined score is your champion pick unless injury or draw path disqualifies them.
